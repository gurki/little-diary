_a little additive tally system, counting to twelve_

[![Terry: twelve tally symbols]({{ '/assets/terry/sequence.svg' | relative_url }})]({{ '/assets/terry/sequence.svg' | relative_url }})

<!--more-->

- **1–9:** left dot, right dot, join into a bar. Repeat upward.
- **10:** connect all three bars with a center stem.
- **11–12:** close the bottom-left, then the bottom-right.

Each count adds ink without removing earlier marks.
