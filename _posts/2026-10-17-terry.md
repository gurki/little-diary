## Background

In many parts of Europe, people commonly use simple strokes to count things. When you get to four, you cross for a full block of five. Simple enough. But oh god are some people terrible at drawing neat lines.

![a mess](https://preview.redd.it/people-in-my-school-dont-know-how-tally-marks-work-v0-xdonkvxcqbte1.jpeg?auto=webp&s=7439622a0ff0bab20017a84ba9b377aaa5d9420e)

At some point I discovered the other systems out there, and the various parts of the world they're being used in. Different systems. Better systems! And on that they I'd found a new hobby. Ever since, I'm introducing anyone who dares draw messy strokes to square tally marks.

![world tally marks](https://roomescapeartist.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Tally-Marks.jpg)

## Hexstora

Some lovely day, I stumbled over a Kickstarter campaign for a modular, 3d printable board game storage system. And there, on a few frames in their latest [promo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZUvK1K4BC8), I spotted them: a couple little dots and lines. Their attempts at creating a compacting, 3d printable tally.

![hexstora tally]({{ '/assets/terry/hexstora-tally.png' | relative_url }})

I loved this! The spark had lit, and I sat down and started expanding on it, and designing my own.

## Exploration

This is the original sketch, trying out various combinations, shapes and forms.

[![original sketch]({{ '/assets/terry/original-sketch.png' | relative_url }})]({{ '/assets/terry/original-sketch.png' | relative_url }})

I wanted something that could be used sequentially, the system should be fully additive. Each count adds ink without removing earlier marks.
This ruled out the triangle variant. And between the rest, the full block felt too boring, the left bars too text-like. I settled on the Kanji-looking center bar and tried a few variants, both digitally and on paper by hand.
Here is the final result:

[![terry: twelve tally symbols]({{ '/assets/terry/sequence.svg' | relative_url }})]({{ '/assets/terry/sequence.svg' | relative_url }})

- **1–9:** left dot, right dot, join into a bar. Repeat upward.
- **10:** connect all three bars with a center stem.
- **11–12:** close the bottom-left, then the bottom-right.

And i love it <3. Hope you do too. Now go count some things!
